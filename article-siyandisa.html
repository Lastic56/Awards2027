Creating Pathways to Opportunity: Siyandisa Achievers Programme

Siyandisa Achievers Programme — Best Woman

About this finalist

CategoryBest Woman
RepresentativeCikizwa Bacela
Edition2027 CSI Legacy Awards
All finalists

For many talented young South Africans, academic ability is not the obstacle to success. Rather, it is the lack of resources, guidance and opportunities that can prevent them from reaching their full potential. The Siyandisa Achievers Programme is working to change this reality, providing high-performing learners from under-resourced communities with the support they need to build successful futures. Its commitment to educational equity and youth development has earned it a place among the finalists in the Best Woman category of the 2027 CSI Legacy Awards.

Established to support academically talented learners from Quintiles 1–3 schools, the Siyandisa Achievers Programme recognises that potential exists in every community, but opportunity is not always equally accessible. Operating across all nine provinces, the programme identifies promising learners in Grade 11 and provides sustained support throughout their educational journey, from high school and university into the world of work.

What began as a scholarship initiative has developed into a comprehensive model of academic, personal and professional development. During its pilot phase, the programme recognised that financial assistance, while important, could not address all the challenges learners faced. Many also needed academic guidance, emotional support, mentorship and access to networks that could help them navigate the journey beyond school. Siyandisa responded by broadening its approach, creating a more holistic support system designed to equip young people for long-term success.

Each year, the programme enrols 50 new learners, offering a range of interventions that include academic assistance, tutoring, psychosocial development, mentorship and expert-led webinars. These opportunities help learners strengthen their academic performance while developing the confidence, knowledge and practical skills needed to make informed decisions about their futures. Importantly, support does not end when learners leave school. Siyandisa continues walking alongside its Achievers through university and into their careers, helping them navigate the transitions that can often determine their future prospects.

The programme's impact is reflected in the 487 Achievers it has supported to date and its engagement with more than 200 schools across South Africa's nine provinces. These figures represent more than participation; they reflect young people being given the tools and encouragement to overcome barriers, pursue further education and build pathways towards meaningful employment and socio-economic mobility.

At the heart of this work is Cikizwa Bacela, whose commitment to creating opportunities for young people is rooted in a belief that circumstance should never determine a person's potential. Her motivation extends beyond academic achievement, focusing on the wider impact that education and access to opportunity can have on individuals, families and communities.

For Bacela, the most rewarding part of her work is witnessing the resilience and growth of the young people she has had the privilege of supporting. She describes her passion as being less about personal achievements and more about the lives she has been able to walk alongside. Seeing Achievers transform their futures, uplift their families and eventually contribute to the communities from which they came is what gives the programme its deeper purpose.

Her vision is also grounded in collaboration. By connecting talented learners with individuals and organisations that have the resources, expertise and networks to support them, Siyandisa helps bridge the divide between opportunity and those who have historically had limited access to it. Building on a decade of impact, the programme is now focused on expanding its reach, strengthening resource mobilisation and developing strategic partnerships to ensure that more deserving learners can benefit.

Being named a finalist in the 2027 CSI Legacy Awards is a meaningful milestone for both Bacela and the Siyandisa Achievers Programme. For Bacela, the nomination reinforces her commitment to advancing educational equity and creating sustainable opportunities for future generations. For the programme, it recognises not only its work, but also the determination and achievements of its learners, alongside the contributions of its partners, donors, mentors and supporters.

Ultimately, Siyandisa's greatest legacy may be found in the futures its Achievers go on to create. By investing in young people and supporting them well beyond the classroom, the programme is helping build a generation equipped not only to succeed individually, but also to strengthen the communities and country they call home.
