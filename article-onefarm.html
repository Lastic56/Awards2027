Connecting the Food System, Transforming Lives: OneFarm Impact

OneFarm Impact NPC — Best Green Impact Initiative

About this finalist

CategoryBest Green Impact Initiative
RepresentativeDr Thembi Xaba
Edition2027 CSI Legacy Awards
All finalists

South Africa faces a striking contradiction: millions of tonnes of perfectly good food are wasted every year, while millions of people struggle to access nutritious meals. At the same time, smallholder farmers often find themselves excluded from formal markets, unable to secure fair prices or reliable routes to consumers. OneFarm Impact NPC is working to address these interconnected challenges by creating a more inclusive, sustainable and resilient agricultural food system. Its work has earned it recognition as a finalist in the Best Green Impact Initiative category of the 2027 CSI Legacy Awards.

Founded and principally funded by Standard Bank Group, OneFarm Impact is an independent non-profit organisation focused on tackling hunger, reducing food waste and supporting the development of smallholder farmers. Its flagship initiative, OneFarm Share, brings together different participants across the agricultural value chain to ensure that food reaches the people who need it, while creating stronger commercial opportunities for farmers.

Launched in 2020 as Standard Bank's emergency response to the market disruptions and rising food insecurity caused by the COVID-19 pandemic, OneFarm Share has since evolved into a national programme operating across all nine provinces. Its model addresses three closely connected challenges: food waste, smallholder farmer development and food insecurity. Rather than treating these as separate issues, the programme recognises that connecting the right people and resources can generate environmental, economic and social benefits simultaneously.

The need for this approach is significant. South Africa discards more than 10 million tonnes of food annually, despite widespread hunger. Meanwhile, many smallholder farmers struggle to access formal markets. An estimated 90% of the country's 2.9 million smallholder farmers have no direct link to these markets, often waiting between 30 and 90 days for payment and losing a substantial proportion of their potential income to intermediaries.

OneFarm Share responds by connecting smallholder farmers with commercial growers, packers, transporters, markets, food distributors, funders and community organisations. Through this integrated system, surplus food that might otherwise go to waste can be recovered and distributed to communities experiencing food insecurity. At the same time, smallholder farmers gain access to markets, fairer commercial opportunities and more reliable payment structures.

The programme's impact since 2020 is substantial. It has paid smallholder farmers more than R88 million and delivered food valued at over R181 million, providing approximately 138 million meals to 1.2 million people. These figures represent more than food distributed or money exchanged. They reflect farmers building viable businesses, rural employment opportunities being created and families gaining access to fresh, nutritious produce.

Among the programme's most meaningful achievements are the stories of farmers who have grown from subsistence production into commercial enterprises, reinvesting their earnings into their farms and expanding their operations. Women farmers have received more than R25 million through the initiative, enabling them to support their families and create employment within their communities. The programme has also seen the wider benefits of consistent access to nutritious meals, including improved school attendance and pass rates in communities where children can rely on receiving food.

Dr Thembi Xaba is associated with the work being recognised through this nomination. The programme's purpose reflects a belief that meaningful development comes from bringing people and resources together to solve problems collectively. Its approach challenges the idea that corporate social investment should simply provide short-term assistance, demonstrating instead how strategic partnerships can build systems that continue delivering value over time.

For OneFarm Impact, being named a finalist in the 2027 CSI Legacy Awards is a tribute to the farmers, commercial growers, packers, transporters, funders and community organisations that make the programme possible. It also reinforces the organisation's belief that South Africa's hunger challenge is not simply a shortage of food, but a failure to connect existing resources with those who need them.

Through OneFarm Share, OneFarm Impact is demonstrating that environmental sustainability, economic inclusion and food security can advance together. Its ambition is not only to deliver meals today, but to help build a fairer food system in which farmers can thrive, communities are nourished and less food is wasted for generations to come.
